# MASTER WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
## LA VIE BELLE | CAFE & BAKERY
### Professional Business Website

Act as a senior web designer, creative director, UX/UI specialist and experienced frontend developer.

Build a complete, premium, production-quality business website for **La Vie Belle (LVB Cafe & Bakery)**, located in Jubail, Saudi Arabia.

# 1. CRITICAL DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

**NO AI-GENERATED IMAGES.**

This is a strict requirement.

- Do not generate images using AI.
- Do not use AI-generated people, cafes, food, drinks or interiors.
- Do not create artificial photographs or fabricated cafe imagery.
- Prioritize genuine photographs supplied from the cafe's official Instagram or by the business owner.
- Use actual cafe photographs wherever available.
- If genuine photographs are unavailable, use tasteful, properly licensed real photography as temporary editorial imagery, without presenting it as photographs of this particular cafe.
- Create elegant image placeholders where actual business photography is required but unavailable.
- Make replacing images straightforward through a centralized assets folder.

Never fabricate the cafe's products, menu, prices, awards, history or customer testimonials.

### Colour palette

Use a sophisticated combination of:

- Warm ivory: #F5F0E7
- Deep espresso: #30251F
- Muted sage: #87917C
- Soft taupe: #C6B9A6
- Charcoal: #242522

Avoid overly pale, washed-out colours.

Use strong contrast, restrained colour accents and generous whitespace.

### Typography

Use an elegant editorial serif font for major headings, paired with a clean contemporary sans-serif for body text.

Suggested fonts:
- Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display for headings.
- DM Sans or Manrope for body text.

Typography should have a clear hierarchy and feel carefully art-directed.

### Overall visual direction

Create a sophisticated European editorial website with:
- Asymmetrical layouts.

- Beautiful typography.
- Delicate dividers.
- Subtle botanical or architectural details.
- Generous spacing.
- Refined hover interactions.
- Elegant transitions.

Avoid:
- Generic AI website layouts.
- Excessive rounded cards.
- Repetitive rectangular sections.
- Unnecessary gradients.
- Excessive animations.
- Emoji decorations.
- Generic coffee cup illustrations.
- Dashboard-like layouts.
